Information flow

From performance to raga

Tonic-independent
  1. Pitch featuresExtract melodic F0 from recording or live microphone.
  2. Sa normalizationConvert each frequency into cents relative to the detected tonic.
  3. Pitch-token quantizationMap normalized contour into the 256-token vocabulary.
  4. Embedding + LSTMModel temporal melodic patterns over fixed 5,000-frame windows.
  5. Classifier + aggregationScore raga windows, then produce recording-level predictions.
